## 3. Summary of Evaluation Weights (Topic F — Agent Wallet)

Per **Doc B (`FINTECHATHON-RULE-C564`, `reviewRule.main[1]`)** and **Doc A (`CMU-IRD-2026`, *Topic F*)**:
1. **Product Completion & Technical Feasibility (`40%`):** Actual completion rate of the working system, technical architecture, deterministic safety gates, and task execution effect.
2. **Commercial Value & Practicality (`30%`):** Market value, practicality, extensibility, efficiency improvement, and alignment with real financial user requirements.
3. **Innovation & Interaction Design (`30%`):** Originality, architectural highlights, conversational interaction design, and user-friendliness.
4. **Topic F Special Challenge Bonus (`+0 to 5 Points`):** Awarded specifically for selecting and executing **Topic F: Agent Wallet (Autonomous On-chain Wallet)** with real on-chain execution and risk control.
